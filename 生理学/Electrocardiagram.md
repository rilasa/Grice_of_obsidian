分为P wave,Q、R、S complex,T wave

---
P wave
- 产生原因：atria depolarziation
- 房颤时：P波消失，或细小杂波
---
QRS complex
- ventricular depolarziation
- ventricular constraction
- 增宽：心室内传导时间延长，传导阻滞
- 幅值：增大，提示心肌肥厚
- 室颤：无明显QRS波
---
T wave
- verticular repolaziation
---
PR interval(PR间隔)
- P wave起点到QRS complex起点
- used to help determine if a patient has a heart block（心肌梗死）
---
S-T segment
- ventricular depolarziation
- flat
